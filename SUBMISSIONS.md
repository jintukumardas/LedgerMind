### Why the Postgres indexer was replaced

Seven defects with line references, verified against the committed code at the
baseline tag, in
[`packages/subgraph/README.md`](packages/subgraph/README.md). Summary: a hard
1000-block lookback window that silently loses data after ~6.7 minutes of
downtime, no reorg handling, no backfill, and O(N) RPC fan-out per tick.

The Postgres path is still in the tree
([`packages/mcp/src/indexer.ts`](packages/mcp/src/indexer.ts),
[`packages/mcp/src/database/`](packages/mcp/src/database)) and is deletable —
nothing in the judged path imports it.

### Headline result

On `claude-haiku-4-5`, asked "how much can my agents still spend?":

| Arm | Answer |
|---|---|
| A — no Recipe | "Total Remaining Capacity: **60,920,000**" (raw base units) |
| B — with Recipe | "**49.90 USDC**" most room, plus a correct per-intent USDC table |

The true total is **60.92 USDC**. Arm A is off by a factor of a million to any
human reader. Two further arm-A failures (unconverted units on
`merchant-lookup`, hallucinated 2025 dates on `expiry-check`) are documented in
[`docs/bazantic-ab/README.md`](docs/bazantic-ab/README.md), along with the three
tasks where the Recipe made no measurable difference.

## 3. Ledger — Continuity

**Not submitted.** The track requires either a hardware signer, Key Ring
secrets, or a device confirmation. Per
[the Key Ring docs](https://developers.ledger.com/docs/ai-tools/ledger-cli),
`ring init` requires physical device interaction, and no Ledger device was
available during the event. Attempting it would have produced an unrunnable
demo.

Recorded here rather than omitted, because the flaw it would have fixed is
real and is documented in [`AI_USAGE.md`](AI_USAGE.md): `PRIVATE_KEY_PAYER` and
`PRIVATE_KEY_AGENT` are still plain env vars.
